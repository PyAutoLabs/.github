# Getting help with PyAutoLabs

Coming from PyAutoFit, PyAutoGalaxy, PyAutoLens, PyAutoArray, or another PyAutoLabs project? The [PyAutoLabs Discussions hub](https://github.com/orgs/PyAutoLabs/discussions) serves all our repositories, workspaces, and tutorials.

Questions, help with your code or your analysis, and ideas: the
[PyAutoLabs Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/PyAutoLabs/discussions).
Bug reports with a reproducer (a snippet, the traceback, your versions):
an issue on the library's tracker. The Slack is for collaborators, by
invitation.

- [Help & Questions](https://github.com/orgs/PyAutoLabs/discussions/categories/help-questions): setup, software use, and scientific analysis.
- [Ideas & Proposals](https://github.com/orgs/PyAutoLabs/discussions/categories/ideas-proposals): suggestions and implementation designs.
- [Bugs & Errors](https://github.com/orgs/PyAutoLabs/discussions/categories/bugs-errors): investigate an error or unexpected result before it is a confirmed reproducible bug.

Include the package/repository, versions, a minimal example, traceback, and expected versus actual behaviour where relevant. Share only information you have permission to make public.

Confirmed reproducible defects and agreed implementation work belong in the affected repository's issues, linked to the originating discussion. Security-sensitive reports should follow that repository's security policy, not a public discussion. If no private reporting route is listed, email James Nightingale at [James.Nightingale@newcastle.ac.uk](mailto:James.Nightingale@newcastle.ac.uk) to arrange one, without including sensitive details in the initial message.

See the [contribution guide](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). PyAutoScientist and AI tools are optional; ordinary questions and non-AI contributions are equally welcome.
